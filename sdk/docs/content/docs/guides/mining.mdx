---
title: Mining
description: Register a hotkey on a subnet, publish your axon, and keep your UID.
---

A miner is a hotkey registered on a subnet, doing whatever work that subnet's
incentive mechanism rewards. The chain side of mining is small: register, tell
validators where to reach you, and don't get evicted. The work itself — the
model, the service, the actual commodity — is defined by each subnet's own
codebase, not by the chain.

## 1. Scope the subnet

```bash
subtensor subnets list
subtensor subnets show 1
subtensor query subnet-registration-cost --netuid 1 --json
subtensor query subnet-hyperparameters --netuid 1 --json   # immunity period, limits
subtensor query metagraph --netuid 1 --json                # who you're competing with
```

## 2. Register

[`burned-register`](/docs/tx/burned-register) burns the subnet's current
registration cost from your coldkey and assigns your hotkey a **UID**:

```bash
subtensor tx burned-register --netuid 1 --dry-run -w my_coldkey
subtensor tx burned-register --netuid 1 -w my_coldkey
```

Know before you send:

- The burned TAO is **recycled, not staked** — deregistering does not refund it.
- The cost floats with demand and is only known at execution time, so a
  configured `Policy` spend cap blocks this call until raised.
- On a full subnet, your registration **evicts the lowest-ranked non-immune
  neuron** — and once your own immunity period ends, the same can happen to you
  if you aren't earning rank.

Confirm with [`uid`](/docs/query/uid) or
[`netuids-for-hotkey`](/docs/query/netuids-for-hotkey).

## 3. Publish your endpoint

Validators find you through the axon info stored on chain.
[`serve-axon`](/docs/tx/serve-axon) writes your `ip:port`; it does **not**
start a server — running the actual service is your job:

```bash
subtensor tx serve-axon --netuid 1 --ip 203.0.113.7 --port 8091 -w my_coldkey -H my_hotkey
```

Use [`serve-axon-tls`](/docs/tx/serve-axon-tls) if peers should verify a TLS
certificate, and [`reset-axon`](/docs/tx/reset-axon) to stop advertising.
Re-publishing is rate-limited by the chain.

## 4. Watch your standing

```bash
subtensor query metagraph --netuid 1 --json     # your rank, incentive, emission
subtensor query blocks-since-last-update --netuid 1 --json
subtensor query immunity-period --netuid 1
```

Rewards accrue to the hotkey as stake. Have them auto-staked to a hotkey of
your choice with [`set-auto-stake`](/docs/tx/set-auto-stake).

## Key hygiene

- Sign operational calls with the hotkey; keep the coldkey off the mining box
  entirely — use a [proxy](/docs/concepts/advanced) if the box must submit
  coldkey-signed calls.
- If the hotkey is compromised, [`swap-hotkey`](/docs/tx/swap-hotkey) replaces
  it while keeping its registrations.
